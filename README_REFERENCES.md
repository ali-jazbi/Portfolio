This folder is a visual design specification for Codex, **not** a completed deployable Next.js project.

- `mobile/`: matching six full-page PNG screenshots (390 px wide)

## Accuracy and publication warnings
- Mock healthcare dashboards, storefronts and survey panels are **illustrative**, not actual workplace screenshots.
- All titles and experience descriptions should be checked before public deployment.
- No synthetic portrait of Ali is used. The About page contains a photo placeholder.
- Parin Sanat start/end dates and Dotr role-change date require confirmation.
- Do not claim measured performance improvements, patient data access, or database architecture ownership.

## Quick setup for Codex
1. Unzip the pack.
2. Put the whole folder under `design-reference/` in your Next.js repository, or give Codex access to its extracted location.
3. Give Codex the instructions from `PROMPT_CODEX_FA.md`.
4. Supply approved real project screenshots later; replace placeholder illustrations.

## Visual language
- Warm white `#F6F6F3`, near black `#171A1D`, restrained blue `#2365EE`.
- Editorial typography, clean geometry, no stock-photo/artificial human portraits or exaggerated metrics.
- Photos and logos must use verified rights, and healthcare images should contain no protected information.
